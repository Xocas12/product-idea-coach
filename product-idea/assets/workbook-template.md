## Product Idea Brief
_[Completed LAST. Up to ~2 pages of prose, active language, simple sentences, no PM jargon (Actor/JTBD), no bullet lists, no visuals. Executive audience. Numbers matter (problem size, TAM, outcomes). Cover the first 4 of the 7 brief parts:]_

- **Problem** — Actor, JTBD, the gap (current → future outcome), key cause(s), problem size.
- **Proposed Solution** — product name, product category, main attributes of the MVP, software/physical deliverables, how it solves the problem.
- **Market Opportunity** — TAM size, the market focus for the MVP, the key competitor, the position-to-own.
- **Value Proposition** — the customer benefits, the pricing, and why customers will find it compelling.
